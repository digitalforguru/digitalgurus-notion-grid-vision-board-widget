FAQ

How do I add this to Notion?

Copy your embed link, go to Notion, type /embed, paste the link, and confirm!

Can I edit this on the builder page?

Yes. The builder page is where you upload, drag, add stickers, customize, and copy your finished widget.

Can people edit it inside Notion?

No. The embed link shows the finished locked board only.
