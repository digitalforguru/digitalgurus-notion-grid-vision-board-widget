FAQ

How do I add this to Notion?

Copy your embed link, go to Notion, type /embed, paste the link, and confirm!

Can I upload my own images?

Yes! Open settings, upload your images, and copy your embed link once your board looks right.

Can I change the layout?

Yes! You can change the title, grid size, widget size, stickers, and collage style.
